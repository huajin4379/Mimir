## ✨ Key Features

### 📝 LaTeX Paper Writing Made Easy
Mimir provides a seamless LaTeX writing experience with live compilation. As you type your paper, Mimir automatically compiles it in the background, showing you the rendered output in real-time. No more switching between editors and compilers or waiting for manual compilation steps.

### 📚 Smart arXiv Literature Management
Keep all your academic papers organized with Mimir's built-in arXiv integration. Search, download, and manage papers directly from arXiv. Mimir automatically extracts metadata and generates proper BibTeX citations, saving you hours of manual reference management.

### 📊 Experiment Tracking Dashboard
Track all your research experiments in one place. Log parameters, metrics, and results for each experiment. Mimir creates beautiful, interactive charts that help you visualize your progress and compare different approaches at a glance.

### 🖥️ GPU Server Management
If you work with remote GPU servers, Mimir simplifies SSH task orchestration. Connect to your servers, submit jobs, monitor resource usage, and manage your computing tasks through an intuitive interface. No more memorizing complex command-line instructions.

### 📈 Interactive Metric Charts
Visualize your research metrics with customizable charts and graphs. Whether you're tracking model accuracy, training loss, or any other metric, Mimir presents your data in clear, actionable visualizations.

## 🎓 How to Use Mimir - A Quick Tour

### Writing Your First LaTeX Paper

1. Open Mimir and click on "New Document"
2. Choose "LaTeX Paper" as your document type
3. Start typing your content in the editor on the left
4. Watch the compiled output appear on the right in real-time
5. When you're done, export your paper as a PDF with one click

### Managing Your References

1. Click on the "Literature" tab in the sidebar
2. Use the search bar to find papers on arXiv
3. Click "Add to Library" for papers you want to save
4. Mimir automatically generates BibTeX entries for each paper
5. Use these entries directly in your LaTeX documents

### Tracking Your Experiments

1. Navigate to the "Experiments" section
2. Click "New Experiment" to start logging
3. Enter your hyperparameters, metrics, and notes
4. Mimir automatically creates charts showing your results
5. Compare different experiments side-by-side

### Connecting to GPU Servers

1. Go to "Servers" in the main menu
2. Click "Add Server"
3. Enter your server's SSH details (address, username, and authentication method)
4. Once connected, you can browse files, submit jobs, and monitor GPU usage
5. Set up automated task schedules for regular computations
